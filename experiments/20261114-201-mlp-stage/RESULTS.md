Measured, unreplicated. One deterministic CPU run, the registered run. No proof, no soundness claim. This result chooses no fix and no design; it is an input for #35 and #32.

Variance standard: the project standard asks for at least five runs. This experiment registered ONE run, because the arithmetic is deterministic, and takes its spread over prompts and positions. ## Answer
- **H-S HOLDS.** Replacing only the SiLU table lookup (step 2^-8, index clipped to [-8, 8)) by s = RNE(2^12 silu(gw / 2^12)) moves the late-layer KL median from 1.466e-3 (M31) to 1.83e-6 nats: ratio 0.00125, registered threshold <= 0.5.
- **H-W DOES NOT HOLD.** Replacing only the 13-bit weights by the float64 weights leaves the KL median at 1.454e-3: ratio 0.992.
- Stated against the registered thresholds only: in abliterated layers 31 to 63, swapping the SiLU table lookup for exact SiLU more than halves the KL median (H-S holds), and swapping the 13-bit weights for the float64 weights does not halve it (H-W does not hold, ratio 0.992). This does not show that the weight conversion contributes nothing, and it is not a split of the error by stage. It is a measured, unreplicated late-layer diagnostic. M31-S is not an admissible operator and not a repair. M31-S also meets PR #124's bar descriptively (1,460 of 1,461 top-1, KL median 1.83e-6, max 1.57e-3); M31 and M31-W do not.
- What the exact-SiLU arm changes: it removes the table's 2^-8 input step and its index clip together, and it still rounds the SiLU output to nearest even. The run does not separate the step from the clip.

## What is NOT established
- M31-S replaces the table lookup by exact SiLU. That removes two things together: the table's input step (gw is floored to the 2^4 grid before the lookup) and the index clip (inputs outside [-8, 8) take the table's end values). It still rounds the SiLU output to nearest even, as the table's entries are rounded. The run does not separate the step from the clip, or either of them from their interaction along the changing residual, and the per-layer residual numbers above do not separate them either.
- That the weight conversion contributes nothing is not established. H-W says only that the float-weight swap does not halve the late-layer KL median here.
- How the error splits between layers 0 to 30 and layers 31 to 63 is not measured.
- M31-S is not an admissible M3 operator: its SiLU input exits the v1 table domain on 180,798 entries and h exceeds the 2^18 honest contract on 2,541 entries; m exceeds the 2,047 class on 101.7M entries in every path (m is not clamped, as in PR #138). No repair (wider domain, clamp semantics, different table) was tested.
- Only layers 31 to 63, abliterated leg, PR #124's 10 prompts, float64 heads/attention/norms. Not the stock leg, not XS18 heads, not layers 0 to 30, not other prompts or hardware.
- One deterministic run; no independent replication. No proof.
