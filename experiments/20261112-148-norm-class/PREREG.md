## Why
PR #138 (merged) found that the M3 design's MLP input m = RNE(2^12 x norm output) is over its public class |m| <= 2,047 on 10.36% of entries in abliterated layers 0 to 29, in every layer (max 142,651 inside its cutoff, 148,536 at its post-cutoff layer 30). The design takes that class from upstream's norm, which clamps to 12 bits (`docs/model.md` 5). Qwen's RMSNorm has a learned gain and no clamp. A statement over real Qwen activations would refuse those inputs. Issue #148 asks which candidate fix keeps every pre-norm output in a class the statement can admit, and what each costs in per-layer error. This lane reports. It does not choose the design arithmetic.

## Hypothesis
At least one of the registered arms C, S, W puts every pre-norm output (both norms, all measured layers, all registered tokens) inside its registered class and also meets the registered per-layer fidelity bar in every measured layer.

The negative (every arm leaves entries out of class or fails the fidelity bar in some layer) is a valid result and would be reported as a design change for #35 / #32 (a committed norm with a range proof).

## Metric
Per (layer i, arm, prompt), over all tokens of the prompt (Frobenius norms), with out = mixer + MLP contribution of the layer and x_{i+1} = x_i + out_REF:
- **r (primary, the bar): layer output relative L2** = ||(x_i + out_arm) - x_{i+1}|| / ||x_{i+1}||. This is the stream the next layer reads, and the quantity PR #124 / PR #138 log as divergence.
- c (descriptive, stricter): the same difference over ||out_REF|| (the layer's own contribution).
- d (descriptive): ||out_arm - out_W|| / ||x_{i+1}||, the arm against the unclamped 2^12 lowering. W carries the MLP lowering's own error (13-bit weights, floors, table); d isolates what the norm-output change adds to it.
- mixer part and MLP part of the difference, each over its own REF norm (descriptive).
Per layer the spread is over the 6 prompts: median, max, min of r, with n = 6 and the token count. Class checks are exact counts with their n.

## Acceptance threshold
Two parts per arm. Both fixed now.
1. **Class.** The arm is IN-CLASS iff no entry of either norm output is outside its registered class in any measured layer on any registered token (C: by construction, clamped count reported; S: |q| <= 2,047 at 2^k* and every recorded shift in 1..24; W: |q| < 2^18).
2. **Fidelity.** A layer passes iff median over prompts of r <= 1e-3 **and** max over prompts of r <= 1e-2 (the issue's example bar). The arm meets the fidelity bar iff every measured layer passes. A guard error on a prompt counts as r = infinity.

An arm PASSES iff it is IN-CLASS and meets the fidelity bar. H holds iff at least one of C, S(k*), W passes.

**How to read each number under the two versions of "a real reply" (DESIGN 10, an open design decision this lane does not make):**
- *Integer-served, exact match* (the reply is generated by the integer model; the proof says each token is that model's top choice). The **class verdict** is the number that matters: an out-of-class input is a position that cannot be proved at all. The fidelity numbers r, c, d do not gate provability under this reading. They say how far one integer layer is from Qwen's float layer, which is a model-quality question for whoever serves the integer model.
- *Float-served, clipped gap* (the reply comes from the float engine; the proof bounds its tokens' scores under the integer model). **Both** verdicts are needed: the class verdict for provability, and the fidelity bar as a necessary per-layer condition for the integer model to stay close to the served float model. It is not sufficient: this lane is open loop and has no logits.
RESULTS states next to every reported number which reading it supports.

## Prior art
In this repo: PR #138 (the finding this lane follows; its MLP lowering and harness are reused), PR #124 (frozen prompts, harness), PR #88 (float64 forward), PR #70 (M3 design and bound ledger, class 2,047 for the public normed input), the norm-scaling lane `experiments/20261110-8-qwen-norm-scaling/` (no registered candidate for the DeltaNet head scaling passed on layer 0), `docs/model.md` 5 (upstream's norm clamps to 2,047 and has no gain), `FEASIBILITY.md` (Qwen's RMSNorm gain 1 + w, judged foldable). Outside: "Massive activations in LLMs" (Sun et al. 2024) reports a few residual channels thousands of times the median that survive normalisation as near-constant large entries; LLM.int8() (Dettmers et al. 2022) found outlier feature dimensions that break a single per-tensor 8-bit activation scale and kept them at higher precision; SmoothQuant (Xiao et al. 2023) moves activation outliers into the weights per channel; clipping-based activation quantisers (PACT, Choi et al. 2018) trade clipping error for resolution. zkML systems with fixed-point activations (EZKL, zkLLM, Sun et al. CCS 2024) pick per-tensor scales and range-check them; none reports a measured public-class boundary for a 27B hybrid model's norm outputs. **New here:** the three class fixes named in the issue (clamp, smaller scale, wider class) measured side by side on both pre-norm outputs of all 64 layers of the abliterated Qwen checkpoint, open loop, with every class check counted.
