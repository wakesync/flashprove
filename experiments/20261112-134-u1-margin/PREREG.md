## Hypothesis
H: on PR #124's 10 frozen prompts (1,461 teacher-forced positions), the output distance between the float64 stock and float64 abliterated references, D = KL(REF_stock || REF_abl), is more than 10 times the head-only integer lowering error E of PR #124 at almost every position and more than 100 times at most positions, so that at the output the lowering error is small next to the stock-vs-abliterated difference. It is falsified by the acceptance rule below.

## Metric
Per position t of every prompt (teacher-forced, logits at t predict token t + 1 given the true prefix; n = 1,461):
- **D(t) = KL(REF_stock(t) || REF_abl(t))** in nats over the full 248,320-token vocabulary, float64, from this lane's own run: both forwards are PR #88's float64 REF (`q1_forward.py`, unchanged) driven by PR #124's `e2e.py` layer loop with only the REF path present, then each checkpoint's final norm, then one streamed pass of `lm_head` (identical in both checkpoints) with PR #124's online log-sum-exp code (`e2e.Stream`, unchanged). The reverse KL(REF_abl || REF_stock) is computed in the same pass and reported (descriptive).
- **E(t)**, read only from files committed on main: E_stock(t) = `kl` of path XS18 in PR #124's `runs/e2e-stock.json`, E_abl(t) = the same in `runs/e2e-abl.json`. **Primary E(t) = max(E_stock(t), E_abl(t))** (conservative: the larger of the two lowering errors). XS20 is reported the same way as a secondary, descriptive comparison.
- **r(t) = D(t) / E(t)**. If E(t) <= 0 (KL at the float floor), r(t) = +inf and the position is listed.
- Reported pooled and per prompt: share of positions with r > 10 and r > 100 (the two registered shares), distribution of log10 r (median, SD, min, max, p1, p10, p90), D median / max, E median / max; per prompt also sum(D) / sum(E) (a per-sequence ratio, descriptive).
- Top-1 table: per prompt, the number of positions where argmax REF_stock != argmax REF_abl (ties to the lowest id, as `e2e.Stream`), with the token ids and REF_stock's top-1 minus top-2 margin; compared with the issue's count of 23 from PR #124's committed `ref_top1`. D at the 3 integer-arm flip records (2 distinct positions: p06a t = 81 and t = 109). Top-1 tokens are reported as ids and decoded text only; the content is not judged.
- Raw per-position D, reverse KL, both top-1 ids and both margins are committed under `runs/`.

## Acceptance threshold
Fixed now, before any D exists. With r(t) = D(t) / max(E_stock(t), E_abl(t)) for XS18:
- **PASS** iff all three hold: (1) pooled share(r > 10) >= 0.95, (2) pooled share(r > 100) >= 0.50, (3) share(r > 10) >= 0.90 on at least 8 of the 10 prompts.
- **FAIL** otherwise. A FAIL with pooled share(r > 10) < 0.50 is labelled "negative in the issue's sense" (on most positions the two references are within 10x of the lowering error); a FAIL with share between 0.50 and 0.95 is labelled "mixed", and the prompts that carry it are named.
- Why these bars: the issue's question is whether the head-only lowering error is "orders of magnitude" below the substitution difference. 10x is the minimum for the error not to blur a difference of the same order; 100x at most positions is "two orders of magnitude" typically. 95% / 90% per prompt allow a small number of positions where the two references nearly coincide (for example deterministic continuations such as repeated tokens or digits), which the per-prompt table will show. No claim is made that these bars are the right ones for a verifier's acceptance rule; that design is out of scope.

## Prior art
In this repo: PR #68 (abliteration localised to out_proj / o_proj and down_proj of layers 17 to 51), PR #87 / #100 / #121 (per-tensor weight binding, the U1 mechanism that does not depend on outputs), PR #88 and PR #124 (head-only integer lowering error E, both checkpoints, 1,461 positions; PR #124 RESULTS section 4 lists this comparison as not measured). Outside: abliteration / refusal-direction ablation (Arditi et al. 2024, "Refusal in language models is mediated by a single direction") changes few weights and is designed to leave ordinary outputs close to the original, which is why the margin is in question. Model-substitution detection from outputs: Model Equality Testing (Gao et al. 2024, MMD over samples), and auditing of substitution in LLM APIs (Cai et al. 2025), which report that output-only tests struggle against close variants such as quantised models. TOPLOC (Ong et al. 2025) commits to activations with tolerance for numerical noise and reports separating model and precision changes from hardware nondeterminism; it is the closest prior idea of "noise floor vs substitution signal". zkML (zkLLM, EZKL) binds the weights inside the proof and does not need an output margin. **New here:** a per-position comparison, on the same prompts and positions, of the stock-vs-abliterated output distance against a measured integer lowering error for this exact model pair.
