## Hypothesis
On real Qwen3.8-27B layer-0 inputs and weights at F = 12, every range-checked cell class of the M2 design stays below 2^18 for all 10 frozen prompts and all 48 V heads, and the integer head output matches the float64 reference to a median relative error of at most 1% per prompt; abliteration leaves every layer-0 tensor and the embedding byte-identical.

## Metric
- **Layout (Q1):** header facts, exact. Mismatch list against M1/M1b/M2 DESIGN shapes.
- **Diff (Q2):** per tensor, sha256 of the byte range [8 + header_len + begin, 8 + header_len + end) in each checkpoint's shard. Identical iff the hashes are equal (and byte length equal). Counts by tensor family and by layer. Coverage = tensors hashed / 1,199, and bytes hashed. Wall time of the streaming pass. Cross-check: for every tensor also fetched in Q3, the sha256 of the fetched bytes must equal the streaming hash (two independent fetches).
- **Fidelity (Q3), per prompt, per head, per F:**
  - state: at the last token, rel_S = ||S_int / 2^F - S_f||_F / ||S_f||_F, and max_S = max over all tokens and cells of |S_int / 2^F - S_f| (absolute, float units).
  - head output o (pre-norm) and gated-norm output y, over all tokens of the prompt: rel_o = ||o_int / 2^F - o_f|| / ||o_f||, rel_y likewise; max_o, max_y absolute.
  - Typical = median over the 48 heads, worst = max over heads, reported per prompt; and over all 480 (prompt, head) pairs: median, min, max.
- **Ranges (Q4):** max |x| per class per (prompt, head, F); count of (prompt, head) pairs with any class >= 2^18; required signed bits per class.
- **Context arm (descriptive, no threshold):** an emulated-BF16 version of the float path (round to BF16 where `modeling_qwen3_5.py` casts: in_proj outputs, conv+SiLU output, beta, core output, norm output) versus the float64 reference, rel_y, so the integer error can be read against the publisher's own precision. Emulation, labelled as such.

## Acceptance threshold
Fixed now:
- **A1 ranges (pre-registered, load-bearing):** at F = 12, 480 / 480 (prompt, head) pairs per checkpoint have every RNG cell class of DESIGN 4.2 (S', M, delta_lo, delta_hi, o, w) below 2^18, every exact limb non-negative and inside its interval, and every public word inside its DESIGN section 2 class. Anything less is FAIL for "the M2 bounds hold on real weights", with the failing classes, prompts and heads named. The unsplit delta (M1 contract) is reported, not thresholded. At F = 14 and 16 the same table is reported; the 2^18 bound was sized for F = 12, so a fail there is expected and is reported as the bits needed, not as a contract fail.
- **A2 fidelity:** an F in {12, 14, 16} is sufficient if, over all 480 (prompt, head) pairs per checkpoint, median rel_y <= 1e-2 and max rel_y <= 5e-2, and the same for rel_S. The needed F is the smallest sufficient one. If none is sufficient, FAIL for A2, with the error source located (input rounding, conv taps at 2^12, gate rounding, or floors) by the ablation below.
- **A3 abliteration:** PASS for "layer 0 identical" if every layer-0 tensor and the embedding are byte-identical between checkpoints; then the layer-0 numbers are identical by construction and the checkpoint arm is reported once, with the identity shown. Any differing layer-0 tensor makes the per-checkpoint numbers load-bearing and both are reported.
- Ablation for A2 (descriptive): at the needed or largest F, rerun with float conv taps (taps at 2^F instead of 2^12) to see how much of the error is tap rounding.

## Prior art
- This repository: M1 (#47) and M1b (#63) range studies and M2 DESIGN (#66), all on synthetic inputs; the feasibility lane (#28) read the same headers for a parameter count; the steering and model-identity work (#36) on synthetic and Maverick-side data.
- Fixed-point fidelity of quantized LLM inference: zkLLM (CCS 2024) and EZKL report integer-vs-float error on real weights for attention / MLP; integer-only transformer inference (I-BERT, Kim et al. 2021) and SmoothQuant (Xiao et al. 2023) study activation ranges and outliers on real checkpoints. For linear-attention / delta-rule state, the FLA library and the Gated DeltaNet paper (Yang, Kautz, Hatamizadeh, ICLR 2025) run the state in FP32 and do not study fixed point.
- Abliteration (Arditi et al. 2024, "Refusal in language models is mediated by a single direction"): orthogonalises residual-writing matrices against one direction, W' = (I - r r^T) W, a rank-1 change per matrix. Model fingerprinting / identity: weight hashing is standard; what is new here is a per-tensor diff of a deployed abliterated checkpoint against its base, and real-weight range and fidelity numbers for the M2 integer contract.
