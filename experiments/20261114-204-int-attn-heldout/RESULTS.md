**Both arms FAIL both registered axes on the eight held-out prompts with the frozen PR #178 scales.**
- **C2047:** H_R FAIL (1 clamped entry: one v entry at layer 23, RNE 2,317 against the clamp 2,047; 0 guard failures). H_F FAIL (13 of 16 layers pass; layers 31, 35, 39 fail on the median, 0.0108 to 0.0127).
- **C1665:** H_R FAIL (4 clamped entries on layers 11, 59, 63; 0 guard failures). H_F FAIL (10 of 16 layers pass; layers 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 59 fail on the median, 0.0100 to 0.0180).

## 1. Plain summary

- PR #178 ran upstream's integer attention (12-bit q, k, v at one power-of-two scale per layer, integer scores, a shifted softmax table, a floor-divided mean) on all 16 full-attention layers of abliterated Qwen3.8-27B, fitting the scales on the same 1,461 tokens it measured. Here the scales were frozen from that fit and the same code ran on eight new prompts (541 tokens: prose, JavaScript, SQL, five-language text, two chat-template prompts, a counting repetition) that nothing was fitted on.
- **Range transfer FAIL in both arms (1 and 4 clamped entries).** At 2,047, one value entry out of 553,984 on layer 23 (SQL prompt, kv head 2) came out 13% over the clamp and was clamped. At 1,665, four entries out of 70.9 million q/k/v entries (16 layers) on layers 11, 59 and 63 (code and prose prompts) were up to 1.9% over and were clamped. Under the registered all-or-nothing rule both arms FAIL range transfer. No arithmetic guard failed.
- **Fidelity FAIL in both arms.** C2047 fails 3 of 16 layers (31, 35, 39) and C1665 fails 6 of 16 (27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 59; layer 59's median is 0.01004 against the registered `<= 0.01`). Pooled medians: 0.00709 (C2047) and 0.00822 (C1665). The failures are on the median; no cell gets near 0.05. Post hoc, descriptive, two different prompt sets, not a paired test and not a registered comparison: C2047's three failing layers are the ones PR #178 reports in sample, with medians within 0.0003 of PR #178's; C1665's failing layers are PR #178's five plus layer 59; PR #178's in-sample pooled medians are 0.00700 and 0.00811 (section 5).
- Read plainly: the registered verdict is FAIL on both axes for both arms. With frozen scales, 1 (C2047) and 4 (C1665) held-out entries exceeded the clamp, and 3 and 6 layers missed the fidelity bar.

## 4. Checks

- **V1 split-reference equality:** the float split equals `q1_forward.attention` bit for bit for all 8 prompts at all 16 attention layers (128 of 128, `runs/attn-timing.jsonl`).
- **V2 determinism:** both arms recomputed as separate calls on h01-prose-short and h05-chat-ordinary at every layer; integer o bit-identical, 64 of 64 (`runs/layers.jsonl` `det_bit_equal`).
- **What V1 and V2 rest on:** both are booleans written by the evaluator during the run (`np.array_equal` in `heldout.py`). The compared arrays, or hashes of them, are not committed, so V1 and V2 cannot be re-derived from the committed files without a model forward; they are backed by unit test (a) and the debug fixture. V3 does have independent support in the committed files: the recorded max |RNE| per (arm, layer, prompt, tensor) follows from the recorded observed maxima and the frozen exponents (a reviewer's descriptive check, https://github.com/wakesync/flashprove/pull/206#issuecomment-6874812234).
- **V3 frozen scales:** every (arm, layer) used exactly the frozen table entry on every prompt (`scales_frozen_ok` 32 of 32, `scales_used` on every prompt row); the summariser re-checks each against the hash-pinned table. Nothing was fitted on the held-out prompts.
- **Sources:** `int_attn.py` sha256 357feafa..., `runs/layers.jsonl` of PR #178 sha256 73404d47..., both checked by the evaluator at start (`runs/progress.log` first record).
- **Evaluator equivalence (before the run, synthetic data):** with exponents fitted in-run by `int_attn.fit`, the frozen-scale evaluator reproduces `int_attn.arm_prompt`'s integer o, de-scaled output and statistics bit for bit; an outlier 100x the evaluation maximum in q, k or v changes no frozen scale and is clamped and counted with its head; the table loader and the summariser refuse malformed, duplicate, missing and unexpected input (32 of 32 tests, `runs/debug/unit-tests.json`). Receipts: `runs/debug/unit-tests.json` belongs to the reviewed head 27b057dd (tests and summariser of commit cc8f8504, 32 tests) and is unchanged; `runs/debug/unit-tests-a1.json` belongs to the PREREG Amendment A1 fix (59 tests: the 32, plus 27 fail-closed tests, 6 of them on a temporary copy of the committed records). `runs/debug/unit-tests-a2.json` belongs to the PREREG Amendment A2 fix (83 tests: the 59, plus 11 guarded-null tests, 6 synthetic descriptive-field tests and 7 on a temporary copy of the committed records). The summariser as amended by A1 and again by A2 reproduces `runs/summary.json` and `runs/summary.md` byte for byte from the committed records.
- **Fixture (debug only, not a registered prompt):** PR #88's "The capital of Italy is" through stock layers 0 to 3 with the frozen layer-3 scales: split bit-equal, determinism and V3 ok (`runs/debug-fixture/fixture.json`).
- **Weights:** 226 stock and 622 abliterated tensors fetched; every sha256 equals PR #124's committed manifests and PR #68's diff.json; 56 embedding rows shared with PR #124's manifest match, 230 rows are new tokens not in PR #124's manifest (`runs/manifest-check.json`).

Vocabulary overlap (56 shared embedding rows, 230 new) is not sequence independence.

## 5. Descriptive, post hoc (written after the registered numbers were read; no bar, not a registered claim)

- The single C2047 clamp (layer 23, h07-code-sql, kv head 2) sits on the layer and prompt of the arm's largest cell, 0.0345 (h07, query head 15, which reads kv head 2). Layer 23 at C2047 has 5 cells over 0.01 against 0 in sample. That is consistent with a clamped value outlier raising one head group's error, but it is one observation.

- Per-prompt pooled medians span 0.0068 to 0.0076 (C2047) and 0.0078 to 0.0090 (C1665): no prompt family stands out (`runs/locations.json` `by_prompt`).
- Comparison with PR #178 (in-sample, on main): the same three C2047 layers fail, with held-out medians 0.01084, 0.01085, 0.01268 against in-sample 0.01081, 0.01072, 0.01294. C1665's extra failing layer 59 has a held-out median of 0.01004 against 0.00961 in sample. This is two different prompt sets, not a paired test.

## 7. What this does not establish

- Anything closed loop: no compounding through later layers, no logits, no token agreement. Open-loop per-head fidelity is not token agreement.
- That eight prompts, 541 tokens, at most 117 tokens long, represent deployment traffic. Longer contexts (PR #178 went to 512 tokens) are not tested held out.
- Any rescue: no per-head or per-channel scale, wider value cell, headroom margin, relaxed bar or new arithmetic was tried.
- The stock checkpoint beyond layer 16; integer projections, q/k norm, rotary, gate or o_proj (all float64 here).
- Any verifier property or admission rule; no proof was made.
- Replication: one deterministic run, unreplicated.
