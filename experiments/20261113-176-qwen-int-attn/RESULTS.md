## 1. Plain summary

- Upstream's integer attention (12-bit q, k, v at power-of-two scales, integer scores, a shifted softmax table, a floor-divided mean) was run on all 16 full-attention layers of abliterated Qwen3.8-27B, open loop, on 1,461 tokens of 10 frozen prompts, and each head's output compared with float64.
- **The ranges hold.** At the in-sample fitted scales no q, k or v entry was clamped on any layer at either clamp (0 of every denominator), no wide value exceeded 32,767, and the largest score was 5,853,070, 0.55% of Smax at 2,047 (1,072,693,504). The sh rule fitted 9 or 10 at 2,047 and 8 to 10 at 1,665, inside the admitted 4..12 with no adjustment on any layer.
- **The accuracy fails the bar on the median (in-sample per-layer scale fit).** Per-layer median rel_y runs from 0.0032 (layer 3) to 0.0129 (layer 39) at 2,047, and from 0.0053 to 0.0180 (layer 43) at 1,665. The per-layer max stays under 0.05 on every layer of both arms (largest 0.0335 at C2047 and 0.0337 at C1665, both at layer 55). The failure is broad, not a few outlier heads: 862 of 3,840 cells are over 0.01 at C2047 and 1,300 at C1665, and at C1665 layer 43 all 240 of 240 pairs are over 0.01.
- **The narrower clamp costs accuracy.** C1665 is never better than C2047 on any layer and fails two more layers. On 4 of 16 layers (19, 31, 35, 39) the fitted exponents came out the same at both clamps (every recorded maximum already fit 1,665 at the same power of two), and there the two arms have the same error; on the other 12 at least one of E_q, E_k, E_v drops by one bit at 1,665.
- Read with the issue's own map: C2047 already fails the bar on 3 layers, so 12-bit attention at these fitted scales is too coarse for Qwen at dh = 256 against this bar, and C1665 is worse again. Either way it is a design input for #35, not a proof result.

## 4. Checks

- **Float split equals PR #88.** The lane's split of `q1_forward.attention` (to get q, k, v and the gate) reproduces `q1_forward.attention`'s output bit for bit on all 10 prompts at all 16 layers (`runs/attn-timing.jsonl`, `mix_bit_equal_q1`), and on the debug fixture.
- **Integer core.** Equal to a pure Python-integer implementation of `docs/model.md` 6 step 4 on 4 synthetic shapes (both clamps, sh 4, 6, 8, 12, GQA ratios 1 to 6) (`runs/debug/synthetic.json`). In the registered run every score and accumulator array was asserted integer-valued and below 2^52 (no assertion fired).
- **Determinism.** C2047 computed a second time on p01 and p09 at every layer: integer o bit-identical, 32 of 32.
- **Weights.** Every fetched tensor's sha256 equals PR #124's committed manifest (stock 226 of 226, abl 622 of 622, embedding rows 319 of 319) and PR #68's `diff.json` (`runs/manifest-check.json`). So the abliterated weights were the ones run for layers 17 to 63.
- **Stops.** None fired. Slot granted 16:40:01Z; MemAvailable at the grant is logged as 13.87 in kB / 1e6 (`runs/progress.log` `memavailable_gb`), about 14.20 GB (decimal) or 13.23 GiB. ## 5. Descriptive, post hoc (written after the registered numbers were read; no bar, not a registered claim)

- **Where it fails.** Every failing layer has a fitted value exponent E_v of 5 or lower, that is a large max |v| (the recorded max |v|, rounded to 4 decimal places, is 27.6994 at layer 27 and between 32.1708 (layer 39) and 133.3838 (layer 63) from layer 31 on, against 6.7523 (layer 15) to 13.8457 (layer 23) at layers 3 to 23; `runs/layers.jsonl` `mx_v`). With one per-layer scale for all value entries, a few large value entries leave the typical entry few bits. But layers 43 to 63 also have E_v 3 to 5 and pass at C2047, so E_v alone does not decide it. The q and k side is not obviously the cause: E_q is 10 or 11 and E_k 6 or 7 throughout. This was not isolated by an arm (for example a q/k-only or v-only lowering) and is a reading, not a finding.
- **Not one prompt.** Cells over 0.01 are spread over all 10 prompts (C2047: 33 on p04-repeat-punct to 133 on p02-ordinary-paragraph, `runs/by-prompt.json`), unlike PR #88's DeltaNet outliers, which sat on p03-repeat-word.
- **After the gate and o_proj.** The attention contribution's relative error (mix rel) is of the same size as rel_y: median over prompts from 0.0005 (layer 3) to 0.0165 (layer 39) at C2047 and up to 0.0306 (layer 43) at C1665. What that does to the residual stream, to later layers or to tokens is not measured (open loop, no logits).

## 6. Deviations from the PREREG

- None in the arithmetic, the arms, the prompts, the metric, the bar, the time stops or the disk stop.
- **Memory units (found in review, after the run).** The PREREG registers "start only with MemAvailable >= 12 GB" and "kill if the process tree RSS exceeds 14 GB". `watchdog.py` and `int_attn.py` (`rss_gb`) divide Linux kB counters (which are KiB) by 1e6 and label the result GB, so every logged memory field (`memavailable_gb`, `peak_tree_rss_gb`, `peak_rss_gb_self`, `rss_limit_gb`) is in units of 1.024 decimal GB. As implemented, the start floor was 12.29 GB decimal (11.44 GiB) and the kill limit 14.34 GB decimal (13.35 GiB). So the implementation did not exactly match the registered rule: read as decimal GB, the floor was 0.29 GB stricter and the kill limit 0.34 GB looser than registered; read as GiB, the floor was looser and the limit stricter. It made no difference to this run under either reading: MemAvailable at the grant was about 14.20 GB (13.23 GiB), above 12, and the process tree peaked at about 8.44 GB (7.86 GiB), sampled every 2 s, far under 14. To convert a logged memory field to decimal GB multiply by 1.024. The disk fields (`disk_free_gb`, bytes / 1e9) are decimal GB as labelled. The logged files are not rewritten, and the two scripts are left as they ran (they are the measurement code of the registered run); a rerun or a reuse should fix the conversion first.
- **Summariser changed after the run** (PREREG Amendments, 2026-11-13): `summarise.py` now refuses duplicate, missing or unexpected records, a missing arm and missing determinism evidence (`test_summarise.py`). Its output on the committed records is byte-identical to the committed `runs/summary.json` and `runs/summary.md`, so no file under `runs/` changed and no verdict changed.
- `runs/run.time` is the `/usr/bin/time -v` output copied from the job's terminal with the clone and scratch paths replaced by `<REPO>` and `<SCRATCH>`; `runs/debug-fixture/watchdog.log` had its paths replaced the same way by `scrub_paths.py` before it was first committed. The watchdog was changed after the fixture to log only command base names (the registered run's log was written by the changed watchdog); the stop logic is unchanged.
- `by_prompt.py` (section 5) is post hoc and descriptive.

## 7. What this does not establish

- Anything closed loop: no compounding through later layers, no logits, no token agreement. A per-head error at the 1% level may or may not move tokens; PR #124-style end-to-end agreement is a later lane.
- Out-of-sample scales: exponents were fitted on the same 1,461 tokens they were measured on (registered, disclosed). A deployment fit on other data could clamp, or be one bit coarser.
- The stock checkpoint beyond layers 0 to 16; other prompts; contexts longer than 512 tokens.
- Integer projections, q/k norm, rotary, gate or o_proj: all float64 here.
- Any verifier property. C1665 passing N4 is arithmetic on the constants; Qwen's attention is still refused elsewhere (dq != d, partial rotary, theta0 = 1e7, dh > 128 for `rotaryServed`; DQ-NE-D.md section 4). No proof was made.
- Whether a different scale rule (per head, per channel, or a wider v cell) would pass: not measured.
