## Why
"An abliterated reply under proof" needs the integer lowering of the DeltaNet heads to produce the same tokens as the real model, end to end. PR #88 found that the per-head bar (max rel_y <= 0.05) fails for XS18 and XS20 at layers 16 and 20, mostly on the repeated-word prompt, while a descriptive stock end-to-end run with XS18 in every DeltaNet layer kept top-1 on 96 of 96 positions of 4 short prompts. The abliterated end-to-end run never finished. Per-head error is a proxy; token agreement and the next-token distribution are the measure that matters for a reply. This lane registers that measure, on both checkpoints, with a bar, on a larger and harder prompt set that includes the prompt where the per-head outliers sit.

## Hypothesis
H1: with every DeltaNet layer's heads (conv through gated norm) replaced by arm XS18, and everything else float64, the model's teacher-forced next-token predictions agree with the float64 reference forward to the acceptance bar below on the frozen prompt set, for **both** the stock and the abliterated checkpoint. H2: the same for arm XS20. Each hypothesis is falsified if its arm misses the bar on either checkpoint.

## Metric
Per checkpoint, per path P in {XS18, XS20, BF16H}, per position: top-1 agreement (argmax of P's logits equals argmax of REF's logits; ties broken by lowest id, identically on both); KL(REF || P) in nats over the full 248,320-token vocabulary, float64, computed by streaming lm_head with online log-sum-exp; max |z_REF - z_P| over the vocabulary divided by REF's logit SD at that position; REF's top-1 minus top-2 logit margin. Reported pooled (n = 1,461) and per prompt: fraction agreeing, KL median / p99 / max, SD over positions, and the per-prompt spread (median, SD, min, max over the 10 per-prompt values).

Descriptive, no threshold (labelled as such in RESULTS):
- **Per-head local error, closed loop.** At every DeltaNet layer, for XS18 and XS20, the float64 heads are also run on the same path's own head inputs, and per (prompt, layer, head) rel_y = ||y_P - y_float|| / ||y_float|| over the prompt's tokens is recorded. RESULTS reports per prompt where the heads over 0.05 sit (layers, heads, count) and sets that next to that prompt's top-1 and KL numbers, to say whether the prompts with per-head outliers are the prompts whose logits move. This is a co-occurrence reading over 10 prompts, not a causal attribution.
- Per layer, per prompt: residual relative difference ||x_P - x_REF|| / ||x_REF||.
- Disagreeing positions are listed with REF's margin.

## Acceptance threshold
A cell (arm, checkpoint) passes iff, over the 1,461 positions, with no guard error on any prompt:
1. top-1 agreement >= 99.0% of positions (at most 14 disagreements), and
2. KL(REF || arm) median <= 1e-4 nats, and
3. KL max <= 0.1 nats.
A guard error (OverflowError) or any other failure of the arm on a prompt counts every position of that prompt as disagreeing with KL = infinity.
- H1 holds iff XS18 passes on stock and on abl. H2 holds iff XS20 passes on stock and on abl.
- Why these bars: 99% top-1 is the brief's bar; top-1 flips at near-ties are expected from any perturbation of the logits, so the bar is not 100%, and every flip is reported with REF's margin. The KL median bar of 1e-4 nats is about 600 times PR #88's descriptive XS18 median (1.6e-7 on 96 positions) and about 20 times the largest per-prompt BF16H median there (5.9e-6), so it is loose for typical positions and fails only if the error is systematic. The max bar of 0.1 nats is about 30 times the largest single-position value in PR #88's 96 positions for both XS18 (0.0025) and BF16H (0.0030), and well below the size of a change that would visibly alter sampling at that position (KL 0.1 nats corresponds to a total variation of up to about 0.22 by Pinsker). Quantisation literature reports whole-model fidelity as perplexity deltas or agreement rates (LLM.int8(), SmoothQuant, GPTQ, AWQ); a per-position max is stricter than those averages. BF16H is reported next to each cell as a yardstick only.

## Kill criterion
- Forward sanity check (as PR #88): REF's top-1 at the last position of p01 must be " Tokyo" (id 25358) on stock and on abl. If not, the forward is broken and no cell is reported as a result.
- Order of work in one heavy job: shared stock layers 0 to 16 (all paths), then **abl** layers 17 to 63 and abl logits (so that the abliterated arm finishes first), then stock layers 17 to 63 and stock logits. Checkpointing: after every layer the full state (every path's residual for every prompt, every log row) is saved to scratch, overwriting the previous one, so a lost session resumes at the next layer with the same code and the same order (resume is recorded in `runs/progress.log`). A resume is not a rerun: the layers already done are not recomputed.
- Budget (from the timing probe, see "Runs", scaled to 1,461 tokens): per DeltaNet layer about 30 s of dense float64 for the three full paths plus about 15 s of head recurrences in parallel, attention layers similar; weight fetch of the next layer overlapped in a background thread; about 64 layers on the abl leg (0 to 16 shared, 17 to 63 abl) and 47 more on the stock leg, about 45 s each, plus two lm_head passes of about 3 to 4 min each: about 95 min unloaded, about 2 h on a loaded host. **Compute hard stop: slot grant + 165 min, and never later than 2026-11-12T02:05Z, whichever is earlier**; a watchdog kills the job by pid then. Anything not finished is reported as not finished. The slot grant time is written first to `runs/progress.log`. These limits do not move after registration.
- If the abl logits are not produced by the hard stop, the lane reports the abl cells as not finished; the prompt set is not shrunk after registration.
- Free disk under 3 GB or RSS over 14 GB: stop, report. Gated or 401/403: stop, no credential search.

## Runs
The forward is deterministic float64 and integer arithmetic; there are no seeds. **Five or more repeated runs are not meaningful for a deterministic forward**: they would give identical numbers. The spread that carries information is over prompts and positions, and that is what is reported (median, SD, min, max over the 1,461 positions and over the 10 per-prompt values). Determinism check instead: in the same job, REF and XS18 are computed a second time for p01, p04 and p09 on both checkpoints as separate paths (REF#2, XS18#2: fresh `Head` objects, separate task in the thread pool, same weights in memory), and their residuals after every layer and their logit metrics must be bit-identical to REF and XS18. A mismatch is reported and makes every cell "not deterministic" (no PASS). This checks the computation, not the weight fetch; the fetch is checked by the sha256 comparison with PR #88's manifests.
- Heavy work only through `fp-heavy.sh`, `taskset -c 0-5`, BLAS threads 6 for the dense matmuls (prompts stacked row-wise per path), head recurrences in a pool of 6 forked worker processes (one task per path and prompt). Results depend on the BLAS thread count at the last-bit level (PR #74 section 1); recorded in ENV.json. Host shared and loaded; recorded.
- No exclusions. Every prompt and position counts.
- **Debug and budget before the registered run (disclosed now).** Before this file: one timing probe on a fixture that is not a registered prompt (256 token ids drawn with numpy `default_rng(12345)` from PR #68's prompt vocabulary), stock layer 0 (DeltaNet) and layer 3 (attention): fetch about 21 s / 16 s per layer; per 256 tokens REF heads 0.8 s, XS18 4.3 s, XS20 4.4 s, BF16H 0.5 s, dense DeltaNet layer 1.7 s, attention layer 2.2 s. No metric was computed. After this file, the new code is debugged only on "The capital of Italy is" ([760, 6511, 314, 14898, 369], not in the suite) through stock layers 0 to 3, and the streaming logit metrics on synthetic logits (numpy `default_rng(0)`) against a direct dense computation. No registered prompt, no layer above 3 and no lm_head row is touched before the registered run.

## Prior art
In this repo: PR #68 (layer 0; abliteration localised to layers 17 to 51 out_proj / o_proj and down_proj), PR #74 (layer 0 norm and state scaling; X3), PR #88 (`qwen-q1-deep-fidelity`, negative: per-head bar fails at layers 16 and 20; descriptive stock end-to-end XS18 96 / 96 top-1 on 4 short prompts; abl end-to-end unfinished). Outside: LLM.int8() (Dettmers et al. 2022), SmoothQuant (Xiao et al. 2023), GPTQ (Frantar et al. 2023) and AWQ (Lin et al. 2024) report whole-model fidelity of quantised LLMs as perplexity and zero-shot accuracy, sometimes token or argmax agreement with the full-precision model; KL to the full-precision distribution is used in later quantisation work as a stricter per-token measure. zkML: zkLLM (Sun et al. CCS 2024) and EZKL report the accuracy of the fixed-point model they prove as perplexity or output agreement on whole models with per-tensor fixed-point scales. Linear-attention and state-space quantisation (e.g. Quamba, 2024) names the recurrent state as the precision-sensitive part. **What is new vs `qwen-q1-deep-fidelity`:** a registered bar (not descriptive), both checkpoints including the abliterated one, both XS18 and XS20, 10 prompts / 1,461 positions instead of 4 / 96, including the repeated-word prompt and three prompts of at least 256 tokens, and a closed-loop per-head error log at every DeltaNet layer to see whether the per-head outliers move the logits.
